Farmer guide

Grown well,listed better.

Four small things decide whether a buyer taps your listing or scrolls past it. Open each one and watch the sample listing change.

  1. Take the photo outside in the morning, before the sun gets harsh. Set the produce on a clean sack or crate and fill the frame. Buyers cannot touch it, so the picture has to do the selling.

    Rule of thumbOne clear photo beats five dark ones.

  2. Say exactly what the price covers: a basket, a bag of 50 kilos, a bunch. Check what the market is charging before you set yours, because buyers compare numbers fast.

    Rule of thumbRound to the nearest 500 naira. It reads honest.

  3. Tell buyers when it came out of the ground. Same day harvests earn the Harvested today tag, and buyers can filter the whole market for today or this week.

    Rule of thumbList the morning you pick, not the week after.

  4. Use clean crates, weigh before you list and label every one. Then choose pickup, delivery or both, so buyers know what to expect before they order.

    Rule of thumbNever list more than you can pack by tomorrow.
